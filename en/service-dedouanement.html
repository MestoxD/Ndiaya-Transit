Customs clearance

Your declarations, without blockage or penalty.

The service

What we take care of.

As a licensed customs broker, we handle all your formalities: tariff classification, declarations, payment of duties and taxes, and release of goods.

Customs clearance is where a well-prepared file saves days — and a poorly prepared one costs demurrage. Our licensed declarants review your documents before the goods arrive, anticipate control points and lodge the declaration as early as possible.

  • Pre-clearance as soon as shipping documents are received
  • HS tariff classification and customs-regime optimisation
  • Declarations on GAINDE / ORBUS, followed through to the Release Order
  • Handling of suspensive regimes, temporary admissions and exemptions
How it works

Three phases, one file under control.

STEP 01

Document review

Checking the B/L, invoice and packing list; identifying any required permits.

STEP 02

Early declaration

HS classification, duty calculation and lodging of the declaration before berthing.

STEP 03

Inspection & release

Assistance during customs inspection, obtaining the Release Order and exiting the port.
